Privacy Policy

What AdSkip does

AdSkip is a Chrome extension that detects ad breaks on supported streaming websites, mutes the audio and speeds up playback during the ad break, then restores normal sound and speed when the programme resumes. All ad detection happens locally in your browser.

Data we collect

  • Free version: we do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your settings and a daily usage counter are stored only on your own device using Chrome's local storage.
  • Pro purchase: payments are processed by Razorpay. Razorpay collects the payment details you enter; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay shares with us your email address, the payment ID and the amount paid, and your name or phone number only if you enter them on the payment page.
  • License check: when you activate Pro, and about once a week afterwards, the extension sends your license key (and nothing else) to our license server to confirm it is valid.

We do not collect your browsing history, what you watch, or any content from the websites you visit.

How we use it

We use your email, payment ID and license key only to deliver your license key, verify your license, handle refunds and answer support requests.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers or any third party, except Razorpay (payment processing) and our hosting providers, and only as needed to run the service, or where the law requires it.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

The use of information received through the Chrome Web Store follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your rights

We keep purchase and license records for as long as your license is active and as long as tax and accounting laws require. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing thrivedigilabs@gmail.com. If we delete your purchase record, your license may stop working.
